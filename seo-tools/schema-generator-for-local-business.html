Schema Generator for Local Business — In Depth

Why Local Businesses Need Schema Markup

LocalBusiness schema markup enables Google to display your business information directly in search results — including your address, phone number, opening hours, price range, and aggregate rating — without users needing to click through to your website. This Knowledge Panel presence significantly increases local search visibility and drives direct engagement from the search results page.

What LocalBusiness Schema Includes

A complete LocalBusiness JSON-LD block includes: @type (the specific business type — Restaurant, MedicalClinic, AutoRepair, etc.), name, address (using PostalAddress), telephone, openingHours, geo coordinates, URL, image, aggregateRating (where applicable), and hasMap. The generator produces all required and recommended fields based on your inputs.

How to Add LocalBusiness Schema to Your Website

Generate your schema markup using the tool, then paste the JSON-LD script block into the head section of every page you want Google to associate with your business — typically your homepage and contact page. No HTML expertise is required. The output is a self-contained script tag you can copy and paste directly into your CMS or HTML file.

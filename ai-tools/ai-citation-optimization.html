AI Citation Optimization — In Depth

What AI Citation Optimization Involves

AI citation optimization — often called AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) or GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) — is the discipline of ensuring that AI engines have accurate, consistent, and complete information about your brand. Unlike traditional SEO which optimizes for keyword ranking, AEO optimizes for the accuracy of AI-generated responses about your brand.

The Four Dimensions of AI Citation Quality

Your AI citation score is built from four measurable dimensions. Recognition is whether the AI engine acknowledges your brand at all. Depth is how much detail the engine can produce about what you do. Accuracy is whether what it says matches reality. Confidence is how definitively the engine describes you versus hedging with uncertainty language. Each dimension has specific optimization levers.

High-Impact AI Citation Optimization Actions

The most effective optimization actions are: publishing factual, cited content about your brand on authoritative third-party domains (press coverage, review sites, industry directories); ensuring your website's core factual claims are in plain, crawlable text rather than JavaScript or images; correcting factual errors that appear in sources AI engines are likely to have trained on; and building consistent entity signals across your web presence.

Logic

Logic

The scanner identifies optimization opportunities by comparing brand-communicated claims against AI-produced narratives, classifying gaps by severity and type, and ranking recommendations by potential score improvement and implementation effort.

Methodology

Methodology

Optimization recommendations are derived from gap analysis between expected and produced AI narratives, weighted by gap severity (Critical, High, Medium), potential awareness point gain, and estimated implementation effort.

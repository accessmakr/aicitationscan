⚡ Quick Answer

AI visibility is the degree to which AI models — ChatGPT, Gemini, Meta AI, Claude, Mistral — accurately recognise, describe, and cite a brand in their generated responses. It's measured across four dimensions: Recognition (does the AI know the brand exists), Depth (how much detail it holds), Accuracy (whether what it states is correct), and Confidence (how certain its language is). A free AI visibility tool runs this test automatically and returns a scored report, typically in under ten seconds.

What AI visibility actually measures

The term is often used loosely — "getting mentioned by AI" or "showing up in ChatGPT answers" — but AI visibility is a more specific, measurable thing than that. A brand can be mentioned by an AI model in a confused, inaccurate, or hedged way that does more damage than good. What actually matters is the quality of the representation, not just the presence of a mention.

There are four things that determine the quality of how an AI model represents a brand:

Recognition

Does the model know the brand exists at all

Zero Recognition means the model has no internal representation of the brand — it doesn't show up in responses at all, even when directly asked. This is the floor that has to exist before any other dimension is meaningful.

Depth

How much specific detail the model holds

A model can recognise that a brand exists while holding almost no useful detail — a vague acknowledgment that could describe hundreds of companies. Depth measures whether the model's representation includes specific, accurate, differentiating information.

Accuracy

Whether what the model states is actually correct

High Recognition and Depth with low Accuracy is a specific and urgent problem: the model knows about the brand and has opinions about it, but some of what it states is factually wrong. This can be more damaging than low visibility.

Confidence

How certain the model's language is

A model can be accurate but express everything about a brand in heavily hedged, uncertain language — "it may be," "reportedly," "some sources suggest." Confidence measures how direct and assured the representation is.

ℹ️
This isn't a citation counter

AI visibility is not measured by counting how many times an AI mentions a brand. Current consumer AI models don't expose that kind of tally — and raw mention count wouldn't capture whether those mentions are accurate, detailed, or confident. The four-dimension score is designed specifically because mention count alone is meaningless.

How an AI visibility tool works

A free AI visibility tool works by sending structured queries about a brand to multiple AI engines and parsing their responses. It doesn't require any integration with the AI engines' training pipelines or any special access — it reads the publicly generated output the same way any user would, and applies a scoring model to what it sees.

Step 1

Submit your domain or brand name

The tool takes a domain or brand name as input and constructs a set of prompts designed to test recognition, depth, and accuracy across different query types.

Step 2

Query multiple AI engines simultaneously

The prompts are sent to each tested engine — Meta AI, Google AI, Mistral, Gemma, and optionally ChatGPT and Claude with your own API key — and the raw responses are collected.

Step 3

Score the responses across four dimensions

Each response is parsed for evidence of Recognition, Depth, Accuracy, and Confidence, and a score from 0–100 is assigned per dimension per engine.

Step 4

Return results and recommendations

The scored profile is returned alongside a Consistency Score (cross-engine agreement) and up to eight specific recommendations for improving the weakest dimensions.

AI visibility vs. AI SEO — not the same thing

These two terms often get conflated, but they describe different problems and need different tools:

AI SEO focuses on optimising content so it appears in AI-generated search results — Google AI Overviews, Bing Copilot, Perplexity — by structuring pages for retrieval and citation. It's an extension of traditional on-page SEO targeting a new type of result.

AI visibility specifically measures how AI models represent a brand internally — not whether a page gets cited in a search result, but whether the model has a coherent, accurate, confident understanding of who the brand is. A brand can rank well for AI SEO while having an inaccurate internal representation, or vice versa.

⚠️
Both matter, but for different reasons

AI SEO affects whether your content gets pulled into AI search results. AI visibility affects what AI models say about your brand when asked directly — in any context, not just search. For brand-conscious companies, both deserve attention independently.

Frequently asked questions

What is AI visibility?
AI visibility is the degree to which AI models accurately recognise, describe, and reference a brand in their generated responses. It's measured across four dimensions — Recognition, Depth, Accuracy, and Confidence — each scored 0-100 per AI engine, plus a cross-engine Consistency Score.
What is an AI visibility tool?
An AI visibility tool tests a brand's domain or name against multiple AI engines and returns a scored profile showing how well each engine recognises and describes the brand. Free tools like AI Citation Scan's Awareness Scan cover Meta AI, Google AI, Mistral, and Gemma with no account required.
What is an AI visibility platform?
An AI visibility platform is a broader product offering multiple capabilities beyond a single scan — scheduled monitoring, historical tracking, team access, and reporting — rather than a single-function checker. Most free tools are tools; enterprise-grade products are platforms.
How do AI visibility tools work?
They send structured prompts about a brand to multiple AI engines, collect the generated responses, and score them across Recognition, Depth, Accuracy, and Confidence dimensions. The scoring parses each response for evidence of how well the model understands and represents the brand.
What is an AI brand visibility tool?
An AI brand visibility tool specifically tests how AI models represent a named brand — as opposed to a page URL or keyword — measuring whether the model knows about the brand, how much detail it holds, and whether what it states is accurate.
What's the difference between an AI visibility tool and an AI visibility platform?
A tool performs a specific function — running a scan and returning a score. A platform wraps multiple functions (scan, track over time, alert, report, manage multiple brands) into a single product. Tools are typically free or low-cost; platforms typically carry a subscription.
Is AI visibility the same as AI SEO?
No. AI SEO targets getting content cited in AI-generated search results. AI visibility measures how AI models represent a brand internally — accurate knowledge of who the brand is, not just whether a page gets pulled into a search result. Both matter, but for different reasons.
Can AI visibility be improved?
Yes, through making accurate, structured, and consistent information about the brand more available across public sources — the same information AI models draw from during training or retrieval. The free scan returns up to eight specific recommendations based on which dimensions score lowest.
How do I check my brand's AI visibility for free?
Paste your domain into the AI Citation Scan Awareness Scan at aicitationscan.com. It tests across Meta AI, Google AI, Mistral, and Gemma simultaneously, with no account or sign-up required, and returns your scored profile in seconds.
What are the best AI visibility products?
The right product depends on what you need. For a first-pass check with no cost, a free tool that covers multiple engines and returns a scored four-dimension profile is the appropriate starting point. For ongoing monitoring or multi-brand management, a paid platform tier makes more sense.
Is AI visibility the same as being cited by AI?
Not exactly. Being cited by AI in a search result is one narrow outcome of AI SEO. AI visibility is broader — it's about whether an AI model, in any context, accurately and confidently represents a brand, including when asked directly rather than in a search query.
Does AI visibility affect AI search results?
Indirectly. A model with stronger internal recognition and depth of a brand is generally more likely to reference it accurately in AI-generated answers. But AI visibility and AI search citation are different mechanisms, and improving one doesn't guarantee improvement in the other.

Logic

Logic

AI visibility is measured through model output — what an AI engine actually generates when asked about a brand — rather than through any special access to training data or model internals. This means the measurement is always a snapshot of current model knowledge, not a guaranteed permanent state.

Methodology

Methodology

Each engine is queried with a structured set of prompts designed to surface Recognition, Depth, Accuracy, and Confidence independently. Responses are parsed and scored 0–100 per dimension. The Consistency Score compares dimension scores across all tested engines for the same brand in the same scan session.

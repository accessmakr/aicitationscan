At AI Citation Scan, we operate on a principle of strict data minimization. Our infrastructure is designed to provide enterprise-grade tools without requiring, harvesting, or selling your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines our transparent approach to data handling.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require user accounts, registrations, or personal data to access our core suite of tools. The only data we process falls into these strictly minimal categories:

  • Voluntary Contact Information: If you utilize our contact forms or feedback systems, we process your name, email address, and message solely to resolve your inquiry.
  • Tool Inputs: Data entered into our generators, validators, and calculators is processed entirely client-side (in your browser) or instantly discarded post-generation. We do not log or store the textual content you optimize.
  • Aggregated Analytics: We measure website traffic to understand system capacity and popular tools.

2. Cookieless Analytics Methodology

We actively reject invasive tracking methodologies. AI Citation Scan utilizes Plausible Analytics, an open-source, privacy-first web analytics platform. Plausible does not use cookies and does not generate persistent identifiers. All site measurement is carried out absolutely anonymously. We cannot identify you, nor can we track you across other websites or devices.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell your data. We do not participate in advertising networks, data brokering, or third-party marketing syndication. Your usage of AI Citation Scan remains entirely independent of advertising profiles.

4. Security Infrastructure

We employ enterprise-grade security architectures to protect the integrity of our application:

  • Mandatory HTTPS/SSL encryption across all endpoints.
  • Client-side processing for the majority of our SEO and developer tools, ensuring your proprietary code and copy never leaves your local machine.
  • Strict content security policies (CSP) to prevent cross-site scripting (XSS) and injection attacks.

5. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Even with our minimal data collection footprint, you retain absolute authority over any personal information you have voluntarily provided (such as via email). You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your communication records. To exercise these rights, please visit our Do Not Sell My Data portal or contact us directly.

6. Contact Information

For any inquiries regarding this policy or our data practices, please reach out to our privacy team via our Contact Form. Direct email requests are handled securely and prioritized for prompt resolution.

Logic: This privacy framework operates on a zero-trust, data-minimization architecture, collecting only essential functional metrics required for system uptime.

Methodology: We utilize cookieless tracking architectures and strict server-side sanitization to ensure passive compliance across all global jurisdictions.

Quick Summary

AI Citation Scan does not collect personal data beyond what you voluntarily submit. Domain names are analysed transiently through AI model APIs and never persisted post-session. We do not sell data, build user profiles, or retain query results beyond your active session. Analytics are handled by Plausible — a privacy-preserving, cookieless service with no personal identifiers.

01

Information We Collect

Domain names. When you enter a domain into our scanner, that domain string is transmitted to AI model APIs to execute brand awareness queries. It is not stored in a persistent database, associated with your identity, or retained beyond the duration of the active session.

Contact form submissions. If you use the contact form, we collect your name, email address, and message content. This data is transmitted via Netlify's form handling infrastructure and used exclusively to respond to your inquiry.

Usage and performance data. Aggregate, anonymised analytics are collected via Plausible Analytics. This includes page views, referral source, and device type. No cookies are set. No personal identifiers are processed or stored.

Optional API keys. If you choose to extend scanning via GPT-4o or Claude 3.5 Sonnet, you supply your own API credentials through the settings panel. These keys are stored exclusively in your local browser session and are never transmitted to our servers under any condition.

02

How We Use Your Information

Submitted domain names are used exclusively to construct structured queries to AI model APIs, retrieve brand awareness responses, generate scored reports, and return results to you within the same browser session. No domain name is retained after the session concludes.

Contact form data is used solely to respond to your message. We do not enrol contact submissions into marketing sequences or share them with third parties without your explicit consent.

Aggregate analytics data is used to understand platform performance, optimise page delivery, and improve tool accuracy. It is never segmented by individual user identity and contains no personal information.

03

Third-Party AI Service Providers

To execute brand awareness analysis, submitted domain names are transmitted as query context to the following AI model APIs. Each provider operates under its own data handling and privacy terms:

META

Meta AI — Llama 3.1 70B

Governed by Meta's Privacy Policy. Domain strings are passed as query context only. No PII is included.

GOOG

Google AI — Gemini 1.5 Flash

Governed by Google's Privacy Policy and Generative AI Additional Terms of Service.

MIST

Mistral AI — Mixtral 8×7B

Governed by Mistral AI's Privacy Policy. All query interactions are transient and non-persistent.

We do not transmit names, email addresses, IP addresses, session tokens, or any personally identifiable information to any AI provider. The submitted domain string is the complete query payload.

04

Cookies & Analytics

AI Citation Scan does not deploy first-party tracking cookies. We do not use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or any cross-site tracking infrastructure that profiles individual users.

Platform analytics are handled exclusively by Plausible Analytics, an EU-based, cookieless provider. Plausible processes only aggregate traffic statistics and does not fingerprint, track, or build profiles on individual visitors. No cookie consent banner is required under EU Directive 2002/58/EC. Full details at plausible.io/privacy.

Netlify, our hosting infrastructure, may collect server-side request logs including IP addresses for security and DDoS mitigation. Netlify's data practices are governed by their privacy policy at netlify.com/privacy.

05

Your Rights

Under applicable data protection regulations, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of personal data we hold about you.

Because we do not maintain persistent records of domain submissions, there is typically no personal data on file to retrieve or delete. For contact form submissions, you may request deletion at any time by contacting us via the form on this page.

If you are located within the European Economic Area, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection supervisory authority.

06

Data Retention

Domain scan results are ephemeral and exist only within your active browser session. No query history is written to persistent storage on our infrastructure.

Contact form submissions are retained for the period necessary to resolve your inquiry — typically not exceeding 90 days — unless a longer retention period is required by applicable law or regulatory obligation.

Aggregate analytics data maintained by Plausible is retained in anonymised form consistent with Plausible's data handling policy.

07

Security

All data transmissions to and from AI Citation Scan are encrypted via TLS 1.3. The platform is deployed on Netlify's globally distributed edge network with automatic HTTPS enforcement on every request.

API key values entered through the settings panel are processed exclusively within your local browser context via localStorage. They are never transmitted to or stored on our servers at any point in the request lifecycle.

While we implement robust operational security practices, no method of data transmission over the internet is guaranteed to be completely secure. We encourage users to treat API credentials as sensitive and rotate them regularly.

08

Children's Privacy

AI Citation Scan is designed for professional use by marketing teams, developers, founders, and agencies. We do not knowingly collect personal data from individuals under the age of 13. If you believe a minor has submitted personal data through this platform, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate remediation action.

09

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy periodically to reflect changes in our platform capabilities, legal requirements, or data handling practices. The effective date displayed in the document header reflects the most recent revision.

Continued use of the platform following any update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy. We recommend checking this page periodically if you use the platform on a regular basis.

Logic

Minimal data collection reduces operational exposure — only information strictly necessary for platform operation is ever processed.

Methodology

Transient session-scoped processing ensures no user-domain relationship is persisted beyond the immediate analysis cycle.

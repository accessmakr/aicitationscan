Privacy Policy

Last Modified: May 2024. AI Citation Scan operates on a "Privacy-by-Design" infrastructure. We prioritize mathematical transparency over data collection.

1. Data Minimization Principle

AI Citation Scan does not require user accounts, email registration, or persistent identity tracking to use our simulation tools. Our technical audits (including GEO simulations and SERP previews) are processed as stateless transactions.

2. Information Collection

We collect only the essential technical data required to maintain service integrity:

  • Simulation Inputs: Content strings entered into tools (e.g., meta descriptions) are processed in real-time and are not stored in our permanent databases.
  • Technical Logs: Standard server logs (IP address, browser type) are kept briefly to mitigate DDoS attacks and are automatically purged.

3. LLM and API Interfacing

When using tools that involve AI citations or LLM visibility audits, your queries may be processed via encrypted APIs. We do not pass personally identifiable information (PII) to these third-party processors.

4. Third-Party Analytics

We avoid invasive marketing trackers. Any analytics utilized are configured for anonymous telemetry only, ensuring that your individual browsing behavior remains private and unlinked to a specific identity.

5. Cookies and Local Storage

We use minimal functional cookies to remember your interface preferences (such as Dark Mode). For a detailed breakdown, please refer to our Cookies Policy.

6. Your Rights (GDPR/CCPA)

Regardless of your location, we honor the principles of data portability and the right to be forgotten. Since we do not store personal profiles, there is typically no data to "delete," but you may contact us at any time regarding technical data concerns.

Governance Inquiries

Questions regarding our clinical data standards can be directed to our technical team.

At AI Citation Scan ("we," "our," or "us"), we are profoundly committed to safeguarding your privacy. This Privacy Policy details our clinical approach to the collection, use, and protection of your data when you visit aicitationscan.com and use our suite of SEO and developer tools.

By accessing our website, you agree to the practices described in this document. If you do not agree with this policy, please discontinue use of our site immediately.

1. Information We Collect

We believe in data minimization. We only collect the information absolutely necessary to provide our services and maintain the security of our infrastructure.

A. Automatically Collected Information

When you access AI Citation Scan, our servers automatically record standard, non-personally identifiable information, which includes:

  • Log Data: Internet Protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, operating system, and timestamp data. This is strictly used for security monitoring and preventing abuse of our APIs.
  • Analytics: We use Plausible Analytics, a privacy-focused, cookie-free analytics tool. Plausible does not collect personal data or track you across devices and websites. It only provides us with aggregated, anonymized insights (e.g., page views, referring websites).

B. Information You Provide Voluntarily

We do not require you to create an account to use our primary tools. However, if you choose to interact with us directly, we may collect:

  • Contact Information: Your name and email address if you submit inquiries through our Contact Form.
  • Feedback Data: Any text or parameters you voluntarily input into our feedback widgets or support tickets.

Note: The text, code, or data you input into our tools (e.g., Text Converters, Meta Generators) is processed client-side or ephemerally on our servers and is never stored or used to train external AI models.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process the minimal data we collect for the following operational purposes:

  • To deliver, operate, and maintain our tools and directory accurately.
  • To analyze aggregated usage trends to improve UI/UX and tool performance.
  • To respond to your customer service inquiries and support requests.
  • To monitor against unauthorized access, malicious bot activity, and DDoS attacks.

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Unlike traditional websites, we do not use marketing, tracking, or invasive third-party cookies. We do not participate in cross-site tracking or programmatic advertising networks that harvest user data.

For exhaustive details on the strictly necessary, localized storage we use (such as saving your dark mode preference), please review our complete Cookies Policy.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may only disclose data under the following strict circumstances:

  • Service Providers: We may share data with trusted infrastructure providers (e.g., hosting, CDN) solely to operate our website. These providers are bound by strict confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal Obligations: We may disclose information if required to do so by law, court order, or to protect the vital legal rights and safety of AI Citation Scan and its users.

5. Data Security

We implement enterprise-grade security measures to protect against the unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction of data.

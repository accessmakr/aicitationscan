Do Not Sell My Data

Your Rights Under the CCPA and CPRA

⏱️ 2 min read •

1. Our Core Stance

At AI Citation Scan, privacy is a fundamental right. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for monetary or other valuable consideration.

2. Your CCPA / CPRA Rights

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), residents of California have the right to request that a business that sells consumers' personal information not sell that personal information. Because we do not sell data, you are inherently protected when using our tools.

3. How We Process Traffic Data

To ensure our directory and tools function optimally, we utilize Plausible Analytics, a privacy-first, lightweight web analytics tool. Plausible does not use cookies and does not collect any personally identifiable information (PII). It tracks aggregate website trends rather than individual visitor behavior.

4. Exercising Your Rights

Even though we do not sell your personal data, if you would like to submit a verifiable consumer request regarding your data rights or seek clarification on our privacy operations, you may contact us at: accessmakr@gmail.com.

Methodology & Curation Logic

Logic: Zero-party data architectures ensure our tools function entirely client-side without relying on user data monetization.

Methodology: We utilize cookie-less analytics to monitor site performance strictly to validate traffic without infringing on CCPA frameworks.

📡 AI Citation Scan

The Mission

Quantifying brand visibility in the age of Generative Search.

Beyond the Blue Link

Traditional search engines directed users to websites. Generative Engines (LLMs) direct users to answers. AI Citation Scan was built to solve the "Invisible Gap"—the space where your brand is mentioned by models like ChatGPT or Gemini, but lacks the structured data or semantic authority to be formally cited.

Our Methodology

01

Curation Logic

We audit content based on "Retrieval Potential." This isn't a directory of links; it's a diagnostic analysis of how well a page satisfies a model's vector-search requirements.

02

Semantic Mapping

Our tools use simulated RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) pipelines to predict which technical assets—metadata, schema, and headers—will be "picked" as a source.

03

Citation Integrity

We cross-reference audits with WCAG 2.1 AA and Schema.org standards, providing a clinical view of your brand's machine-readability score.

Transparency & Citations

All audit benchmarks provided by AI Citation Scan are derived from the latest public documentation from OpenAI, Google DeepMind, and Anthropic. We believe in an open web where brands are cited fairly based on the quality and accessibility of their data.

Our clinical methodology

AI Visibility Scan combines multi-page crawling, structured data extraction, and advanced scoring across five master categories to produce the most actionable AI + SEO audit available in 2026.

  1. Crawling & data collection – Full-site discovery respecting robots.txt, JavaScript rendering, and screenshot capture for visual context.
  2. AI discoverability analysis – Regex detection of definition-style content, how-to language, listicles, FAQ patterns, chunkability metrics, and citation likelihood signals.
  3. Technical SEO health – robots.txt, sitemap, indexability signals, canonicals, Core Web Vitals, architecture depth, and orphan page estimation.
  4. Content quality & freshness – Word-count distribution, heading hierarchy, internal linking density, thin-content detection, and recency heuristics.
  5. Entity, schema & trust scoring – JSON-LD parsing, Open Graph, E-E-A-T page presence (About, Contact, Privacy, etc.), and authoritative tone signals.
  6. Weighted master scoring & clinical synthesis – Five scores are blended into an overall 0–100 rating. Audit Engine then writes the 10-section report with strategic recommendations and a full step-by-step implementation guide.

Result: A consultant-level audit you can act on immediately — without the consultant price tag.

Cookies Policy

Last Updated:

At AI Citation Scan, we prioritize user privacy and technical transparency. Unlike most SEO platforms, our infrastructure is designed to operate without intrusive tracking mechanisms.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. They are typically used to "remember" users or track behavior across websites for advertising purposes.

Zero Marketing Cookie Commitment

AI Citation Scan does not use advertising cookies, third-party tracking pixels (like Meta or Google Ads), or behavioral profiling scripts. We do not sell your data.

2. How we use cookies

We use zero non-essential cookies. The only tracking technology present on this site is Plausible Analytics.

  • Plausible Analytics: A privacy-first, cookieless analytics tool. It allows us to see how many people visit the site without collecting personal data or using persistent identifiers.
  • Functional Storage: We may use local browser storage to remember your "Dark Mode" preference or tool settings. This data never leaves your device.

3. Third-party services

While we do not use marketing cookies, some embedded tools (like GitHub-hosted scripts or CDN-delivered fonts) may perform basic logging required for security and delivery. These are strictly technical and do not track you for marketing.

4. Managing your preferences

Since we do not use tracking or marketing cookies, there is no "Opt-Out" required for privacy. However, you can choose to block all cookies in your browser settings without losing any functionality on this site.

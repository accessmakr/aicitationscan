Welcome to a tracker-free environment. Unlike the vast majority of digital properties, AI Citation Scan operates under a strict "No Marketing Cookies" mandate. This policy details how we handle local storage and why your browser remains uncluttered when you visit our platform.

1. What We Do Not Use

We believe your digital footprint is yours to own. To support this, we have systematically removed conventional tracking mechanisms from our infrastructure:

  • No Marketing Cookies: We do not deploy retargeting pixels, advertising tags, or demographic profilers.
  • No Third-Party Trackers: We block data syndicates and advertising networks from monitoring your interactions on our domain.
  • No Intrusive Consent Banners: Because we do not use invasive cookies, we do not require you to navigate complex, deceptive cookie consent banners.

2. How We Measure Traffic (Plausible)

To maintain our platform and understand which tools are delivering value, we require basic site analytics. We exclusively use Plausible Analytics. This is a privacy-first analytics tool designed to comply with global privacy regulations inherently.

Plausible works by counting aggregate trends without generating personal profiles. It does not use cookies, and it does not store persistent identifiers in your browser. All metrics are localized and fully anonymized upon collection.

3. Essential Functional Storage

While we avoid cookies, certain web tools may utilize your browser's native `localStorage` strictly to save your UI preferences (such as dark mode toggles or recent tool settings). This data never leaves your device, is never transmitted to our servers, and is used entirely to enhance your local usability.

4. Managing Your Preferences

Because any stored data is purely functional and kept entirely on your device, you have complete control over it. You can clear your browser's cache or `localStorage` at any time without impacting your core ability to use our toolset.

Logic: Implementing a cookieless architecture removes the technical friction of consent banners while providing superior page speed and user trust.

Methodology: We execute our analytics tracking exclusively through the Plausible JS script, ensuring no unique identifiers or payload data violates user privacy.

Citations: See the Plausible Analytics Data Policy for technical confirmation of cookieless operations.

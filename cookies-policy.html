Cookies & Data Policy

Surgical Privacy Standards — Last Updated: May 2026

At AI Citation Scan, we believe that data integrity is the foundation of AI visibility. Our platform is engineered to audit Generative Engine performance without compromising user privacy. We do not use intrusive tracking mechanisms because technical excellence does not require surveillance.

1. The Zero-Tracking Protocol

We do not use advertising cookies, third-party tracking pixels, or behavioral profiling scripts. This site is built to be "Lighthouse-Safe" and "Privacy-Clean," ensuring no data is leaked to third-party ad networks (e.g., Meta, Google Ads).

Plausible Analytics (Cookieless)

We use Plausible Analytics to measure site performance. This tool is 100% privacy-friendly and does not use cookies. It collects aggregate data only—no personal identifiers are stored, and no "fingerprinting" is performed.

2. Audit Data & Ephemerality

When you use our GEO Audit tools or SERP Simulators, the URLs and metadata you input are processed in real-time to generate your report. We do not "sell" your audit history or the technical weak points discovered during your scan to third-party competitors.

3. Local Storage

We may use your browser's localStorage to remember your UI preferences (such as Dark Mode) and temporary tool inputs. This data remains on your machine and is never transmitted to our servers for marketing purposes.

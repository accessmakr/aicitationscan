Website Tech Stack Checker for Shopify — In Depth

What the Shopify Tech Stack Checker Detects

The checker identifies: the active Shopify theme name and whether it is a premium theme from the Shopify Theme Store or a custom build, Shopify Plus plan indicators, installed third-party apps that inject scripts into the storefront (reviews, loyalty, upsell, and analytics apps), CDN configuration, payment provider scripts (Stripe, PayPal, Klarna, etc.), and live chat and customer support tools.

How to Research a Competitor Shopify Store

Enter the competitor store's URL into the checker. Within seconds you will see the full technology profile: which theme they use (and whether you can purchase the same one), which apps power their reviews, upsell, and loyalty features, and which analytics tools they have deployed. This intelligence informs your own Shopify technology decisions directly.

Identifying Shopify vs Shopify Plus Stores

Shopify Plus stores often use custom checkout scripts, specific CDN configurations, and enterprise app integrations that are detectable in the page source. The checker identifies Plus-specific patterns where they are publicly exposed, helping you understand whether a competitor operates on the standard or enterprise plan.

Logic

Logic

Shopify technology detection uses HTTP response header analysis combined with Shopify-specific DOM signature matching — including Shopify CDN paths, theme asset URLs, and app-injected script patterns.

Methodology

Methodology

The checker identifies Shopify stores via the myshopify.com CDN patterns present in asset URLs, then extracts theme identifiers from stylesheet paths and app signatures from injected scripts in the page source.

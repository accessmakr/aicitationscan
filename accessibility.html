Accessibility Statement

Our commitment to an inclusive and usable digital experience.

⏱️ 3 min read •

1. Our Commitment

AI Citation Scan is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities. We are continually improving the user experience for everyone and applying the relevant accessibility standards to guarantee our platform serves as an inclusive environment for developers, SEO professionals, and content creators.

2. Conformance Status

The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) defines requirements for designers and developers to improve accessibility for people with disabilities. It defines three levels of conformance: Level A, Level AA, and Level AAA. AI Citation Scan is partially conformant with WCAG 2.1 level AA.

Partially conformant means that some parts of the content may not fully conform to the accessibility standard. However, we consistently audit our core UI components to ensure:

  • Color contrast ratios meet or exceed the 4.5:1 standard for regular text.
  • Full keyboard navigability for interactive elements and form inputs.
  • Proper semantic HTML structuring, including ARIA labels where necessary, for screen reader compatibility.

3. Assistive Technologies

Accessibility of AI Citation Scan relies on the following technologies to work with the particular combination of web browser and any assistive technologies or plugins installed on your computer:

  • HTML
  • WAI-ARIA
  • CSS
  • JavaScript

4. Feedback & Continuous Improvement

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of our site. If you encounter accessibility barriers, please reach out to us. We try to respond to feedback within 5 business days.

Please contact us at: accessmakr@gmail.com.

Methodology & Curation Logic

Logic: Semantic HTML and strictly enforced ARIA patterns allow assistive devices to interpret data clusters natively.

Methodology: Automated Lighthouse audits and manual keyboard navigability testing are executed before deploying any master UI changes.

Citations: Guidelines adopted from W3C Web Accessibility Initiative (WAI).
